Scale your Aiven for Valkey™ service vertically or horizontally, and tune performance and
memory to keep it running efficiently as it grows.

- **Vertical scaling**: Change your service plan to move to a node with more memory and
  compute.
- **Horizontal scaling**: Use [clustering](/docs/products/valkey/concepts/valkey-cluster)
  to shard your data across multiple nodes, increasing capacity and throughput beyond
  what a single node can provide.
