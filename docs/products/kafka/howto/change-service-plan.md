Change the service plan for your Aiven for Apache Kafka® service to scale resources up or down and optimize costs.

Plan changes apply to Kafka services that use a service plan, including
[Classic Kafka](/docs/products/kafka/classic-kafka-overview), Developer, and Free
tier services.

To change the plan for a Standard Kafka service, see
[Change the plan for your Standard Kafka service](/docs/products/kafka/howto/change-standard-kafka-plan).
