Change the service plan for your Aiven for OpenSearch® service to scale resources up or down and optimize costs.

<ChangePlan/>

Changing the plan replaces the nodes of your service using the same procedure as a
maintenance update. For the steps, the effect on your clients, and how to prepare them,
see
[What happens during a maintenance update](/docs/products/opensearch/howto/maintenance-updates#what-happens-during-a-maintenance-update).
