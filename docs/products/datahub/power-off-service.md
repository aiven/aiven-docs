You can power off an Aiven for DataHub service at any time to stop all processes and reduce costs.
Powering off a DataHub service also powers off its component services.

Metadata ingestion stops and you cannot access the DataHub UI or API.
Ingestion restarts and access is restored when you power the service back on.
