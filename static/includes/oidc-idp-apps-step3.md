## Step 3: Configure the identity provider in the Aiven Console

1. In the Aiven Console tab, enter a name for this identity provider.
1. Paste the **Issuer/Provider URL**, **Client ID**,
   and **Client secret** you copied from your identity provider.
1. Optional: Enter additional custom scopes.
1. Click **Add**.
