1. In the Aiven Console, go to your Runtime application and click <ConsoleLabel name="runtimeidp"/>.
1. Click **Add authentication method**.
1. Select **OpenID Connect (OIDC)** and click **Next**.
1. Enter a name, and paste the **Issuer/Provider URL**, **Client ID**, and **Client secret** you copied from your identity provider.
1. Copy the **Redirect URI**.
1. Click **Add**.
